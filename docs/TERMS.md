# Terms of use — draft

**Status: draft, unpublished.** This is the text the terms page must serve. No
domain or hosting exists for this project; see `docs/RELEASE.md`.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-22, against the shipped behaviour.

These terms describe what Arc 21 actually does. Where a clause could be written
either as a promise or as a description, it is written as a description —
a term that overstates what the software guarantees is worse than no term.

---

## 1. What Arc 21 is

Arc 21 is a **simulated card game**. It simulates blackjack. Virtual chips have
no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes.

It is **not** a gambling service. No money enters or leaves the app. There is
no wagering with real currency, no cash-out, no purchase, no loot box, no prize
wheel, no side bet and no paid advantage. There is nothing to buy.

Arc 21 is intended for people aged **18 or over**.

---

## 2. Virtual chips

Virtual chips are a score. They are not money, not a currency, not a stored
balance and not property.

- They have **no monetary value** and are never exchangeable for money, goods,
  prizes or anything of value.
- They **cannot be bought.** There is no purchase path in the software.
- They **cannot be transferred** to another player, traded or gifted.
- They are **not withdrawable** in any form.
- They exist only inside your account and only while your account exists.
  Deleting your account destroys them.

If your bankroll falls below the smallest table's buy-in, a Recovery Grant
restores it. This exists so that running out cannot strand you, and because
there is nothing to sell you instead.

Private tables, tournaments and the Daily Challenge use **isolated session
chips** that never touch your main bankroll. That is an anti-collusion measure,
not a limitation.

---

## 3. Your account

You may play Practice, the strategy guide and the drills with no account at
all, offline.

An account is needed for anything recorded on a server: the Daily Challenge,
tournaments, public and private tables, friends and leaderboards.

You agree to:

- give an email address you control;
- keep your password to yourself;
- choose a username and display name that are not abusive, impersonating or
  deliberately misleading;
- be 18 or over.

One person, one account. Running several accounts to influence a leaderboard,
a tournament or a private table is the sort of thing section 5 is about.

---

## 4. What the game guarantees, and what it does not

**The shuffle is fair.** Cards are shuffled with a cryptographically secure
random number generator using an unbiased algorithm, dealt in the order they
were shuffled into the shoe, and never influenced by whether you are winning,
losing, how long you have played, your level, or anything else about you. There
is no hidden difficulty setting. The Fair Play screen in the app describes this
in detail.

**That is a statement about the process, not a promise about your session.**
Blackjack is dominated by variance in the short run. You can play every hand
correctly and lose for a long time.

**The strategy advice is computed, not copied**, by an expected-value solver
run against these exact rules. Where the tables have no answer, you are shown
nothing rather than a guess. It is advice about a simulated game. **Playing
well here does not improve your chances of winning money anywhere else, and no
strategy makes blackjack profitable.**

**Arc 21 has not been tested or certified by any external laboratory, regulator
or auditor.** No such audit, certification or approval exists, and nothing in
the app or its listing claims one.

---

## 5. What you may not do

- Modify, instrument or reverse-engineer the app in order to influence a game
  outcome, a chip balance, a leaderboard position, a tournament result or
  another player's session. The server decides all of these and will reject
  attempts, but doing it is still a breach of these terms.
- Use more than one account to affect a competitive result, or coordinate with
  another player to do so.
- Automate play — bots, scripted clients, replay tools.
- Abuse, harass or impersonate another player through a display name, username
  or bio.
- Attempt to disrupt the service for other players.

We may suspend or remove an account that does these things.

---

## 6. Availability

Arc 21 is provided as it is. Practice works offline and is always available.
Anything server-backed depends on a network and on the service being up, and
neither is promised. A server-backed session can be interrupted; the game is
designed so that an interruption does not corrupt your state or lose settled
chips, but an interrupted session is still an interrupted session.

The service, its rules, its modes and these terms may change.

---

## 7. Liability

To the extent the law allows, Arc 21 is provided without warranties, and
liability is limited to the amount you paid for it — which is nothing, because
it cannot be paid for.

Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

---

## 8. Ending it

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. It is immediate and it
cannot be undone. Deletion is refused only while you are sitting at an active
table, so that leaving does not strand the other players.

We may end your access if you breach these terms.
